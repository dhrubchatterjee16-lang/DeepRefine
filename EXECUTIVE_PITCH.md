## 1. The Multi-Million Dollar Problem in Refining
A typical 100,000 barrel-per-day (BPD) atmospheric refinery generates **\$2.5B–\$3.0B in annual revenue** on thin crack spread margins (\$6–\$12/bbl). In this high-volume environment:
- **Unplanned Rotating Equipment Trips:** A single wet gas compressor or crude charge pump breakdown costs **\$12,500 to \$38,000 per hour** in lost throughput and flaring penalties. Average unplanned turnaround: 48–72 hours (\$1.5M+ direct loss).
- **Sub-Optimal Energy & Yield Give-Away:** Operating distillation columns and FCC risers sub-optimally wastes 3–5% in furnace fuel gas and loses high-value cuts (kerosene/diesel) to low-value bottoms.
- **Operator Alarm Fatigue:** Control rooms receive thousands of nuisance alarms daily during crude switches, leading to delayed human reaction times.
